📘 University GRC Platform: Role-Based Operational BlueprintVersion: 1.0 (Finalized for Implementation)Date: June 12, 2026Focus: Daily Utility, AI Augmentation, and Regulatory Compliance (CERT-In/DPDP)

This document defines the daily operational reality for each of the 9 finalized roles. It moves beyond "assignment tracking" to continuous risk management, ensuring the platform is opened every day, not just during audit season.

Super AdminMission: Platform Integrity & Global ConfigurationDaily Frequency: Low (As-needed) | Criticality: High

🛠 Daily Work & MonitoringTenant Health Monitoring: Oversee uptime and data sync status across all university departments.Global RBAC Audits: Review logs for "privilege escalation" attempts or unusual admin activity across the institution.Retention Policy Enforcement: Ensure automated purging of old evidence logs complies with data sovereignty laws.🤖 AI & RAG AugmentationAnomaly Detection Agent: AI analyzes global access patterns to flag suspicious admin behavior (e.g., mass role changes at 2 AM) and auto-locks accounts pending review.Config Copilot: RAG suggests optimal platform settings based on the university’s specific regulatory mix (e.g., "Enable FERPA-specific logging modules for US-based research grants").🖥️ Dashboard & PagesGlobal Health Map: Real-time status of all department nodes (Green/Amber/Red).Audit Trail Explorer: Immutable log of all super-admin actions.Data Sovereignty Map: Visualizes where data is stored vs. where regulations require it to be.💡 Strategy for Daily Use"Silent Guardian" Mode: The system runs automated self-checks daily. The Super Admin only logs in when the AI Anomaly Agent flags a critical security event, making the tool a safety net rather than a chore.

2. Institution AdminMission: Organizational Onboarding & Risk AppetiteDaily Frequency: Medium (Weekly Reviews) | Criticality: High

🛠 Daily Work & MonitoringDepartment Onboarding: Map new research centers/departments to the platform and assign initial Department Reviewers.Risk Threshold Setting: Define acceptable risk levels for different domains (e.g., "Zero tolerance for Student Data leaks," "Medium tolerance for IT downtime").Executive Reporting: Generate high-level risk summaries for the Vice-Chancellor/Board.🤖 AI & RAG AugmentationOrg-Chart Sync: AI parses HR/Student Info System exports to automatically suggest role updates (e.g., "Detected 50 new 'Principal Investigators'; assign to 'Dept Reviewer' role?").Predictive Risk Heatmap: AI aggregates data from all departments to predict top 5 emerging institutional risks before they become incidents.🖥️ Dashboard & PagesInstitution Risk Scorecard: Single view of compliance health across all departments.User Lifecycle Manager: Bulk approve/reject role assignments suggested by AI.Executive Briefing Generator: One-click PDF/PPT creation for board meetings.💡 Strategy for Daily Use"Morning Coffee" Digest: Instead of logging in to search, the Admin receives a daily AI-generated email summarizing: "3 Departments dropped below 90% compliance; 2 High-Risk Vendors added." Clicking the link takes them directly to the fix.

3. Compliance Officer (Core User)Mission: Regulatory Translation & Control OperationsDaily Frequency: High (Daily Driver) | Criticality: Critical

🛠 Daily Work & MonitoringRegulatory Mapping: Translate new laws (e.g., DPDP amendments) into specific internal controls.Evidence Triage: Review daily control failures flagged by the system and distinguish between "true risks" and "false positives."Gap Management: Assign remediation tasks to Department Reviewers based on gap analysis.🤖 AI & RAG AugmentationRegulatory Change Agent: RAG ingests new government gazettes and automatically drafts a Gap Analysis Report against current policies.Smart Triage: AI pre-sorts daily alerts by severity, suggesting: "This MFA failure is likely a false positive due to known network outage; archive?"🖥️ Dashboard & PagesControl Task Manager: Kanban board of assigned controls (To Do, In Progress, Evidence Uploaded, Verified).Gap Assessment Matrix: Visual view of compliance gaps by framework (ISO, NIST, DPDP).Evidence Verification Queue: Streamlined interface to approve/reject uploaded evidence with comments.💡 Strategy for Daily Use"Zero-Inbox" Goal: The AI handles 80% of routine queries and false positives. The Officer’s daily work focuses only on the top 10 critical exceptions that require human judgment, making the tool feel like a productivity booster, not a task list.

4. IT Security OfficerMission: Technical Controls & Incident Response (CERT-In)Daily Frequency: High (Real-Time) | Criticality: Critical

🛠 Daily Work & MonitoringIncident Response (CERT-In): Log incidents immediately upon detection; manage the 6-hour reporting countdown.Vulnerability Management: Review daily scans for unpatched systems or misconfigurations.Technical Evidence Validation: Verify that automated controls (MFA, Encryption) are functioning via API logs.🤖 AI & RAG AugmentationCERT-In Drafting Agent: As the timer ticks, AI auto-drafts the mandatory incident report (email/portal format) based on initial logs, saving critical minutes.Auto-Remediation Scripts: AI suggests (or executes) Terraform/CLI scripts to fix common cloud misconfigurations (e.g., "S3 bucket public → Click to Privatize").🖥️ Dashboard & PagesIncident Command Center: Live timer for active incidents, checklist for CERT-In/DPDP steps, and communication log.Technical Control Health: Real-time pass/fail status of IT-specific controls (NIST Annex A).Vulnerability Register: Prioritized list of CVEs affecting university assets.💡 Strategy for Daily Use"The 6-Hour Shield": The tool’s primary value prop for this role is liability protection. By automating the CERT-In clock and report drafting, it becomes the only safe way to handle incidents, ensuring daily reliance.

5. AuditorMission: Independent Assurance & ReportingDaily Frequency: Medium (Seasonal Peaks) | Criticality: High

🛠 Daily Work & MonitoringEvidence Sampling: Review evidence packages submitted by departments.Observation Logging: Document findings, exceptions, and recommendations directly against controls.Report Finalization: Compile audit reports for management sign-off.🤖 AI & RAG AugmentationSmart Sampling: AI analyzes 100% of transactions and recommends a statistically significant sample for review, highlighting high-risk outliers.Drafting Assistant: RAG accesses prior year’s workpapers to auto-draft "Observation" and "Risk" sections, which the auditor refines.🖥️ Dashboard & PagesAudit Workspace: Read-only view of controls, evidence, and logs with a side panel for adding comments.Finding Tracker: Status of open/closed audit observations.Report Builder: Drag-and-drop interface to assemble final audit documents.💡 Strategy for Daily Use"Risk-Focused Assurance": Shift from random sampling to AI-guided risk sampling.  Auditors use the tool daily during audits because it shows them exactly where the problems are, reducing their workload by 50%.

6. Department Reviewer (Lab Manager, HR, etc.)Mission: Local Compliance & Evidence SubmissionDaily Frequency: Medium (Task-Driven) | Criticality: Medium

🛠 Daily Work & MonitoringTask Execution: Complete assigned control tasks (e.g., "Review access logs," "Update policy").Evidence Upload: Submit proofs (screenshots, logs, signed forms) for their specific department.Self-Assessment: Answer periodic questionnaires about local risks.🤖 AI & RAG AugmentationPlain-English Translator: Converts complex compliance jargon (e.g., "NIST 800-171 3.5.4") into simple tasks: "Upload a screenshot showing MFA is on for your lab computers."Pre-Flight Check: AI scans uploaded files instantly to verify they contain required data (e.g., "Missing date on training log") before submission, preventing rejections.🖥️ Dashboard & PagesMy Department Dashboard: Simple view of their own compliance score and pending tasks.Task Wizard: Step-by-step guided interface for completing controls.Evidence Vault: History of all submitted files and their status.💡 Strategy for Daily Use"No-Jargon Zone": By translating compliance into plain language and preventing errors before submission, the tool reduces frustration. The AI Pre-Flight Check gives immediate positive feedback, encouraging daily engagement.

7. Vendor ReviewerMission: Third-Party Risk Management (DPDP)Daily Frequency: Medium | Criticality: High

🛠 Daily Work & MonitoringVendor Onboarding: Assess new vendors using standardized questionnaires (HECVAT).Contract Review: Verify DPDP clauses (data processing, breach notification) in vendor contracts.Continuous Monitoring: Track vendor risk scores and certification expiries (SOC 2, ISO).🤖 AI & RAG AugmentationContract Analyzer: RAG ingests lengthy SOC 2 reports/contracts and instantly summarizes High-Risk findings or missing DPDP clauses.Breach Watch: AI scans news/dark web for mentions of critical vendors and alerts the reviewer if a vendor suffers a breach.🖥️ Dashboard & PagesVendor Risk Register: List of all vendors with risk scores (Low/Med/High/Critical).Assessment Workspace: Interface to review vendor responses and upload contracts.Expiry Tracker: Alerts for expiring certifications or contracts.💡 Strategy for Daily Use"Automated Due Diligence": Instead of manually reading 100-page SOC 2 reports, the reviewer uses the AI Contract Analyzer to get a summary in seconds. This speed makes the tool indispensable for processing new vendors quickly.

8. Policy Approver (General Counsel, VP)Mission: Governance & Sign-OffDaily Frequency: Low (Event-Driven) | Criticality: High

🛠 Daily Work & MonitoringPolicy Review: Review and digitally sign off on new or updated policies.Conflict Resolution: Ensure new policies do not contradict existing ones.Impact Assessment: Understand who and what processes are affected by policy changes.🤖 AI & RAG AugmentationConflict Detector: AI scans the new policy against all existing policies to flag contradictions (e.g., "Conflicts with 2024 Travel Policy, Section 4").Executive Summary Generator: RAG creates a one-page brief explaining why the policy is changing and who it affects, based on the draft text.🖥️ Dashboard & PagesPolicy Inbox: List of policies pending approval.Diff Viewer: Side-by-side comparison of current vs. proposed policy versions.Approval History: Log of all signed policies with timestamps.💡 Strategy for Daily Use"One-Click Approval": Senior leaders are busy. The Diff Viewer + AI Summary allows them to understand and approve policies in <5 minutes, removing the bottleneck of "waiting for sign-off."

9. Read-Only Assessor (Board, Regulators)Mission: Oversight & VisibilityDaily Frequency: Low (Periodic) | Criticality: Medium

🛠 Daily Work & MonitoringDashboard Review: View high-level compliance and risk metrics.Report Consumption: Access final audit reports and risk digests.Trend Analysis: Observe long-term risk trends without interacting with data.🤖 AI & RAG AugmentationConversational Dashboard: Natural language querying: "Show me FERPA incidents in Engineering over the last 6 months" or "Why is ISO readiness down 5%?"Auto-Briefing: AI generates a weekly "Risk Digest" email summarizing top 3 emerging risks.🖥️ Dashboard & PagesExecutive View: Read-only, high-level charts and heatmaps.Report Library: Secure access to finalized audit and compliance reports.Q&A Interface: Chatbot for asking questions about the data.💡 Strategy for Daily Use"Insight on Demand": Instead of static PDFs, the Conversational Dashboard allows them to ask specific questions and get instant, cited answers, making the tool a powerful decision-support system.🚀 Implementation Strategy: The "Daily Hook"To ensure this is a daily utility and not just an "audit assignment tool":

Start with the "Pain Killers": Launch with the CERT-In Incident Module (IT Security) and AI Policy Diff (Approvers). These solve immediate, high-stress legal problems that must be solved daily/quickly.Integrate, Don't Isolate: Connect to HR (Workday), Student Info (Banner), and Cloud (AWS/Azure) immediately. If users have to manually upload data for basic controls, they will hate the tool. Automation is the key to daily adoption.The "Morning Digest" Email: Every role gets a personalized, AI-generated email every morning at 8:00 AM with their Top 3 Actions. This pulls them into the platform daily without them having to remember to log in.Gamify Compliance: Show departments their "Compliance Score" publicly (internally). Healthy competition between departments drives daily engagement.
